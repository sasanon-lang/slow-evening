5 Evening Habits for Deeper Sleep

Updated August 2026

You do not need a two-hour night routine to sleep better. Most nights, five small habits are enough to tell your body the day is over.

1. Dim the lights after 8 pm

Bright overhead light keeps your brain in daytime mode. Switch to a lamp or warm light in the evening and melatonin does its job on schedule.

2. Put your phone in another room

Willpower is not a plan. Charging your phone outside the bedroom removes the decision entirely.

3. Stretch for five quiet minutes

Nothing athletic. A slow forward fold and a few deep breaths lower your heart rate and mark the transition to rest.

4. Write down tomorrow's top task

Racing thoughts at night are usually unfinished plans. Writing one line about tomorrow lets your brain stop rehearsing it.

5. Keep your bedroom slightly cool

Your core temperature needs to drop to enter deep sleep. A slightly cool room makes that easier.
