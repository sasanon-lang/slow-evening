A 30-Minute Wind-Down Routine

The internet loves elaborate night routines. Busy weeknights do not. This one takes 30 minutes, needs nothing you do not already own, and survives the nights when you get home late.

Step 1: Close the screens (minutes 0 to 10)

Ten minutes before your routine starts, close the laptop and park your phone on its charger, ideally outside the bedroom. The routine begins when the feed ends.

Step 2: Warm water (minutes 10 to 20)

A warm shower, or just washing your face if you are exhausted. The warmth and the ritual both signal the switch from day to night, and the cooldown afterward helps your body drift toward sleep.

Step 3: Tea and a few pages (minutes 20 to 30)

Herbal tea and five pages of a paper book. Not a productivity book, nothing with a highlighter. Fiction, essays, anything that asks nothing of you.

Step 4: Lights out at the same time

The unglamorous secret of good sleepers: the same lights-out time, most nights. Your body learns the rhythm and starts getting sleepy on schedule, which makes every other step easier.

What you might need

Probably nothing: a kettle and any paper book will do. Two small things help the routine stick:

  • A caffeine-free herbal tea sampler, so step 3 never gets boring.
  • A simple analog alarm clock, so your phone can spend the night in another room without costing you your morning alarm.

When you only have ten minutes

Skip the shower and the tea. Phone out of the bedroom, face washed, lights out on time. A shortened routine kept is worth more than a perfect routine skipped.
